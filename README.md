# SparkCity Wallet 🚀

**AI-Powered Real-Time City Commerce Platform**  
SparkCity Wallet is a smart city wallet that helps local businesses attract nearby customers using real-time AI-generated offers based on weather, time, location, and live demand signals.

---

## 🌍 Problem Statement

Local businesses often struggle to compete with large e-commerce platforms that use personalization, dynamic pricing, and advanced analytics.

Traditional coupon systems are:

- Static  
- Poorly timed  
- Generic  
- Low conversion  

Small merchants need intelligent tools to bring in customers at the right moment.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

SparkCity Wallet transforms city commerce through AI.

It generates **hyper-personalized real-time offers** for users based on live context such as:

- 🌦 Weather conditions  
- 🕒 Time of day  
- 📍 User proximity  
- 🏪 Merchant foot traffic  
- 🎉 Nearby events  
- 📈 Demand trends  

### Example:

> Cold lunch hour + nearby café has low traffic

AI generates:

**☕ Warm Cappuccino 80m away – 20% off for next 12 mins**
